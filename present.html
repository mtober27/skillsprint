Short lessons you can finish in one sitting. A quiz at the end. Your progress stays in the browser.

The loop

Watch. Answer. Then the quiz.

1. Pick topicsBusiness, finance, technology, science, history, communication.
2. Open a pathLessons unlock in order. Twelve paths, twenty-seven lessons.
3. The clip pausesTwo checks in the video. You answer before it continues.
4. Take the quizFeedback shows immediately, with the reason.

Try one from the app

Which question belongs in a business model?

Two scores

XP levels you up. Coins are for the shop.

XP

Lessons, clip checks, and challenges. This is your level.

Coins

20 for a perfect lesson. 8 for any other first clear. 12 for a daily challenge. 5 more when a streak continues.

Streak

A day counts when you finish a lesson or a challenge. Practice runs do not pay coins.

Shop

Three things to buy.

Streak shield · 30Miss one day and the streak stays. You can hold two.
Hint · 15On one quiz question, a wrong answer is hidden.
Light · 40Ice or Ember changes the cursor light. Warm is free.

The code

React on the page. Vite behind it.

ReactEach screen is a component: dashboard, lesson, quiz, shop.
JavaScript dataLessons and questions sit in files. The screen reads them.
localStorageName, XP, coins, and streak are saved in the browser. No server.
ViteThe tool that runs the app while it is being built, then packages the files the site serves.
